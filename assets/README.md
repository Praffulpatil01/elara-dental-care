# Assets & Imagery Documentation — Elara Dental Care Demo

This directory contains the visual and vector assets used in the Elara Dental Care website redesign concept.

---

## 1. Vector Assets (SVG)

All vector artwork was custom-designed for this demonstration using clean mathematical curves and the brand's bespoke palette:
- **`favicon.svg`**: Minimalist icon combining a stylized tooth and a gentle health leaf arc with gold accents.
- **`logo.svg`**: Horizontal brand mark pairing the geometric emblem with editorial serif wordmark.
- **`icons/consultation.svg`**: Clinical inspection probe & oral examination mirror symbol.
- **`icons/root-canal.svg`**: Endodontic tooth canal preservation symbol.
- **`icons/cleaning.svg`**: Prophylaxis scaling and radiant enamel sparkle symbol.
- **`icons/restorative.svg`**: Conservative composite restoration inlay symbol.
- **`icons/orthodontics.svg`**: Orthodontic alignment brackets and smile archwire symbol.
- **`icons/implants.svg`**: Precision titanium dental implant foundation symbol.
- **`icons/whitening.svg`**: Enamel brightening and luminescence rays symbol.
- **`icons/cosmetic.svg`**: Aesthetic smile harmony and veneer contour symbol.
- **`images/doctor-placeholder.svg`**: Dignified silhouette portrait placeholder clearly labeled for Dr. Nikita More until an official clinic photo is provided.
- **`images/map-preview.svg`**: Styled neighborhood map showing Prabhadevi, Century Bazaar, Veer Savarkar Marg, and Dr Annie Besant Road connectivity.

---

## 2. Photographic Assets

Photographic assets in `assets/images/` are selected to reflect a welcoming, modern, and clinical healthcare atmosphere:
- **`hero-smile.jpg`**:
  - *Usage*: Hero Section main visual.
  - *Art Direction*: Warm, authentic, healthy adult smile with natural lighting and depth of field. Represents the gentle, patient-centric mission of Elara Dental Care.
  - *Licensing & Fallback*: Editorial style, high-resolution web-optimized JPEG.
- **`clinic-interior.jpg`**:
  - *Usage*: Patient Comfort Section (primary interior).
  - *Art Direction*: Spotless modern dental consultation suite featuring light oak finishes, an ergonomic treatment chair, fiddle-leaf plant, and soothing sage-green wall palette.
- **`patient-consultation.jpg`**:
  - *Usage*: Patient Comfort Section (secondary overlay).
  - *Art Direction*: Unhurried, attentive consultation showing a dentist explaining anatomical smile models with a patient. Labeled as a representative clinical environment.
